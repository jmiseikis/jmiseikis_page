# Privacy Policy

Last updated: [publication date]

This website, jmiseikis.com, is run by Dr. Justinas Mišeikis, based in Zurich, Switzerland
("I", "me"). This policy explains what personal data the website processes, why, and what
choices you have. It is written for the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where
it applies to visitors in the EU or EEA, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Who is responsible

Dr. Justinas Mišeikis, Zurich, Switzerland.
Contact for privacy matters: dr.miseikis@gmail.com

## The short version

- No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies are used on this website.
- If you use the contact form, your details are used to reply to you and for nothing else.
- Some pages load content from third parties (fonts, directory data, video thumbnails), which
  means those providers receive your IP address when your browser requests the content.

## What is processed, and why

### Visiting the website

When you open a page, the hosting and backend providers process technical data that every web
request contains: IP address, browser and device type, the page requested, the referring page
and the time. This is needed to deliver the website, keep it secure and investigate errors.
These logs are kept by the providers for a limited period under their own retention rules.

### The contact form

If you send a message, I receive your name, email address, optional organisation and your
message. The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that the submission comes
from a person and not an automated script. Your IP address is used briefly to limit repeated
submissions and may appear in short-lived error logs.

The message is not stored in a website database. It is delivered by email to my inbox through
the email service Resend. I use it to answer your enquiry and, if we start working together,
for that engagement. I delete enquiries that do not lead to further contact when they are no
longer needed, and keep business correspondence for as long as legal record-keeping rules
require.

Legal basis (GDPR): steps taken at your request before a possible contract, and my legitimate
interest in answering enquiries.

### Content loaded from third parties

To show certain content, your browser connects directly to these services, which receive your
IP address and standard browser information:

- **Google Fonts** (Google): the Inter typeface.
- **Google Sheets** (Google): the data behind the robotics, investor and events directories.
- **YouTube image servers** (Google): video thumbnails on the Speaking page.

The list of recent videos is fetched by the website's server from the YouTube API, without any
visitor data. Links to YouTube, LinkedIn, Google Scholar, Google Patents and other external
sites only send data to those sites when you click them, and their own privacy policies then
apply.

Legal basis (GDPR): my legitimate interest in presenting the website's content reliably.

### Information stored in your browser

The website does not set analytics or advertising cookies. It may store small technical
preferences in your browser, such as your choice of light or dark theme. These stay on your
device and can be cleared in your browser settings. Cloudflare Turnstile processes browser
signals to tell people and bots apart when the contact form is shown.

### Swiss Tech Pulse (discontinued)

The website previously offered the Swiss Tech Pulse digest, which has been discontinued. No new
subscriptions are accepted, no further digests are sent, and no subscriber data is held.

## Service providers and international transfers

I use these providers to run the website. They process data on my behalf or as independent
providers of the services described above:

| Provider | Purpose | Location of processing |
|---|---|---|
| Lovable and its cloud backend (Supabase) | Website hosting, backend functions | EU and/or USA |
| Cloudflare | Spam protection (Turnstile) | Global network, including the USA |
| Resend | Delivery of contact-form emails | USA |
| Google | Fonts, directory data, video thumbnails, email inbox | Global, including the USA |

Where data is transferred to a country without an adequate level of data protection as
recognised by Switzerland or the EU, the transfer relies on recognised safeguards, such as the
Swiss-US and EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or standard
contractual clauses.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict its
processing, and, where applicable, receive it in a portable format. Use the contact details
above. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority: in Switzerland, the
Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU, the authority in your
country of residence.

No automated decision-making or profiling takes place on this website.

## Changes

I update this policy when the website changes in a way that affects personal data. The date at
the top shows the latest version.
