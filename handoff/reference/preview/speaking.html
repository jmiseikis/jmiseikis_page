Physical AI: from demonstration to deployment

Technology leaders, industrial teams and investors

A practical view of what it takes to move embodied AI into real operating environments.

  • Distinguish technical demonstrations from deployment readiness
  • Identify integration, reliability and operational questions
  • Evaluate a realistic path from pilot to use
02

Corporate-startup collaboration that moves forward

Innovation teams, founders and partnership leaders

How to align technical ambition, business priorities and the practical work of a partnership.

  • Define a useful pilot and a shared decision process
  • Recognise mismatched incentives and expectations
  • Plan the transition from exploration to implementation
03

Evaluating AI and robotics beyond the pitch

Investors, executives and corporate strategy teams

The technical and commercial questions that make an AI or robotics opportunity easier to assess.

  • Separate capability claims from supporting evidence
  • Connect technical risks with commercial assumptions
  • Ask better questions before committing resources
