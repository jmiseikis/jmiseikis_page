Blank working template | 6 October 2026

THESIS: use case, technical capability, customer problem and strategic rationale.
SCOPE: geography, early stage or scale-up, product maturity, company/lab/partner type, exclusions.
EVIDENCE: what qualifies a candidate? Sources, customer proof, evaluation method, technical dependencies and open questions.
SHORTLIST ROW: name; source URL; checked date; stage evidence; technical fit; commercial fit; key uncertainty; reason to approach; public contact route; relationship status.
PRIORITIES: must-have criteria, useful extras, disqualifiers and largest uncertainty.
ENGAGEMENT: partnership, pilot, supplier evaluation or investment assessment.
OUTREACH: concise reason to speak, proposed question and desired next step. Introductions require agreement from the participants.
DELIVERY: decision owner, deadline, review cadence and next validation steps.

A listing or funding announcement is a discovery signal, not diligence or proof that a company is currently raising.
