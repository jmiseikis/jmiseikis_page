A map by purpose

Who helps with which question?

This is a selective navigation guide, not a ranking or a complete company database. Zurich is one entry point; EPFL and the national innovation network broaden the view beyond one region.

01 / Research & emerging capabilities

What technical expertise or new approach could matter to us?

ETH AI Center ↗

An entry point for ETH’s AI research activity and events.

Your next step: Identify the relevant research group, then check publications, capabilities and the route for an industry enquiry.

02 / University startup discovery

Which companies are emerging from research?

EPFL Startup Launchpad ↗

A university startup hub with a public community and startup directory.

Your next step: Screen for your use case and stage. A university origin is a research signal, not proof of commercial readiness.

03 / Applied R&D & collaboration

Where could a company find a relevant research environment?

Switzerland Innovation ↗

A national network connecting companies, researchers and academic partners.

Your next step: Match a specific technical need to a park or specialist facility; verify capabilities, terms and available capacity.

04 / Innovation support

Which support route might fit a collaborative project?

Innosuisse ↗

The Swiss Innovation Agency supports innovation projects, collaboration and startup development.

Your next step: Check the current programme and eligibility with the agency before building it into a funding or market-entry plan.

For venture investors

Early stage and scale-up need different evidence.

Early stage: test the technical thesis

  • What works beyond a controlled demo?
  • Which data, IP and specialist skills are essential?
  • What experiment would reduce the largest uncertainty?
  • Which customer problem justifies the next milestone?

Scale-up: test repeatability

  • Can performance repeat across customer deployments?
  • How much custom engineering does each sale require?
  • What are the support, infrastructure and hardware costs?
  • Can the team sustain delivery, reliability and growth?
