Scope the assessment around your decision.

Agree the depth, access to evidence, timetable and deliverables before work begins. Technical review of IP does not replace a legal opinion; specialist reviews can be defined as part of the broader process.

For venture investors

Match the diligence to the company’s stage.

Early stage

Understand the technical thesis, the quality of the evidence and the work needed to reach the next milestone.

  • Reproducible demo and evaluation method
  • Data access, dependencies and IP provenance
  • Team capability and critical hiring gaps
  • Validation plan and commercial assumptions

Scale-up stage

Assess repeatable delivery, deployment economics and the constraints that emerge as the business grows.

  • Performance across real customer environments
  • Integration effort and customer-specific work
  • Support, reliability and operating costs
  • Technical roadmap and scaling bottlenecks
