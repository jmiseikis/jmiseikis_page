Research, strategy and the work between them.

My background combines technology scouting at Sony, strategy and partnerships at Sony AI, and AI leadership at F&P Robotics, alongside a PhD in robotics and computer vision and an Executive MBA.

That combination shapes how I approach a technical claim, a partnership opportunity or a research-to-market decision: understand the evidence, connect it to the business context and define a practical next step.

Based in Switzerland, I work with international teams in English. I also host TechDrive Zürich, speaking with founders, researchers and operators across the Swiss technology ecosystem.
