An example planning sequence

What should the first 90 days establish?

Use this as a planning framework. Timing depends on the product, market, procurement cycle and availability of people to speak with.

  1. Days 1–30

    Define where you can win

    Compare a small set of segments and markets. Map buyer needs, alternatives, differentiation and the local proof required. Build an initial company and partner shortlist.

    Decision: Which segment and route deserve validation?

  2. Days 31–60

    Test the route with relevant people

    Prepare discovery questions, approach target accounts and potential partners, and document feedback. Refine the message, pricing assumptions and proposed pilot.

    Decision: Is there a credible buyer problem and a workable route to serve it?

  3. Days 61–90

    Set up the next commercial test

    Define a pilot or partner-validation brief with an owner, success criteria, support model and follow-up. Review unresolved commercial and delivery dependencies.

    Decision: Proceed, narrow the scope, change the approach or stop?
