TECHNOLOGY MARKET-ENTRY BRIEF
Dr. Justinas Mišeikis | jmiseikis.com/swiss-baltic-market-entry
Blank working template | 6 October 2026

PRODUCT: capability, evidence of value, current markets and commercial maturity.
MARKET HYPOTHESIS: country, customer segment, buyer role, buying trigger and problem.
ALTERNATIVES: current customer process, competitors and reason to switch.
ENTRY ROUTE: direct sales, integrator, distributor, research collaboration or other partnership. Specify responsibilities and incentives.
LOCAL REQUIREMENTS: language, integration, service response, procurement evidence, product requirements and specialist legal/regulatory questions to resolve with appropriate advisers.
SHORTLIST: organisation, role, reason for fit, evidence URL/date, known gap, public contact route, relationship status (public lead / existing relationship / introduction consented).
VALIDATION: questions for customers/partners, evidence required, feedback and next experiment.
PILOT: problem, scope, buyer/sponsor, delivery owner, timing, success criteria, commercial next step.
RESOURCES: budget, team time, travel, local support and opportunity cost.
DECISION: proceed / narrow / change / stop; evidence, owner and review date.

Working framework, not a promise of meetings, sales or market access.
